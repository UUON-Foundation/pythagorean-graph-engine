# Support UUON Foundation

Every engine in this organization is free, open, and runs in your browser without install, login, or subscription. Building and maintaining this work takes real time.

If it has been useful to you — for research, for learning, for building something of your own — consider supporting it directly.

---

## Ways to contribute

**Ko-fi** — one-time or monthly, no fees taken by the platform

> [ko-fi.com/uuonfoundation](https://ko-fi.com/uuonfoundation)

**GitHub Sponsors** — recurring support directly through GitHub

> [github.com/sponsors/UUON-Foundation](https://github.com/sponsors/UUON-Foundation)

**Direct contact** — for commercial licensing, consulting, or partnership

> phi1@uuonfoundation.com

---

## What support goes toward

- Research and development of new geometry engines
- Documentation, academic records, and provenance infrastructure
- API layer development for F=(P,E,R,C) services
- Keeping all engines free and public

---

## What you get

Nothing is paywalled. Every engine published here remains free regardless of whether you contribute. Support is voluntary and appreciated, not required.
